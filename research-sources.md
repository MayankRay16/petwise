# Petwise care-source cross-check (September 2026)

- AVMA pet care: https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare — responsible ownership, preventive care, safety, medications, and veterinary partnership.
- AVMA vaccinations: https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare/vaccinations — vaccination plans should be individualized by a veterinarian based on circumstances and needs; urgent reactions include difficulty breathing, collapse, persistent vomiting/diarrhea, hives, and facial swelling.
- AAHA preventive care: https://www.aaha.org/resources/pet-health-resources/preventive-care/ — preventive care resources cover wellness exams, vaccines, nutrition, and life-stage assessments.
- ASPCA emergency care: https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/general-pet-care/emergency-care-your-pet — emergency signs include pale gums, rapid breathing, difficulty standing, loss of consciousness, seizures, and excessive bleeding.
- ASPCA companion-animal species policy: https://www.aspca.org/about-us/aspca-policy-and-position-statements/species-suitable-be-companion-animals — lists dogs, cats, horses, rabbits, ferrets, birds, guinea pigs, select small mammals, small reptiles, fish, and legally/responsibly kept domestic-bred farm animals as companion categories; opposes wild animals and wild/domestic hybrids.
- Merck Veterinary Manual: https://www.merckvetmanual.com/ — authoritative veterinary reference across species.
- RSPCA pet advice: https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets — welfare-first companion-animal advice.
- Humane Society pet care: https://www.humanesociety.org/resources/pet-care — everyday care, adoption, and behavior resources.
- CDC healthy pets: https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-pets/about/index.html — zoonoses, hygiene, and public-health guidance.
- Companion Animal Parasite Council: https://capcvet.org/ — parasite prevention guidance.
- American Heartworm Society: https://www.heartwormsociety.org/ — heartworm prevention and education.
- WSAVA guidelines: https://wsava.org/global-guidelines/ — global veterinary guidelines for companion animals.
- Association of Shelter Veterinarians: https://www.sheltervet.org/guidelines-for-standards-of-care-in-animal-shelters — evidence-informed shelter-animal care standards.
- AKC dog breeds: https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/ — official AKC breed list; source confirms examples such as Bernese Mountain Dog, Pug, and Siberian Husky.
- CFA cat breeds: https://cfa.org/breeds/ — CFA recognized cat breeds.
- ARBA rabbit breeds: https://arba.net/recognized-breeds/ — recognized rabbit breeds.
- Livestock Conservancy poultry breeds: https://livestockconservancy.org/heritage-breeds/poultry-breeds/ — chicken, turkey, duck, and goose breed resources.
